AL-MIZAN

The Price of Creation

A Novel by Asif M. Shah

“The scale does not ask what you want. The scale does not care what you need. The scale weighs, and the weight is always exact, and the exactness is the cruelty.” — From the Codex of the Order of the Mizan, author unknown

“But the alchemists who vanished into the desert three hundred years ago believed they had found a way to tip the scale. They were wrong. The scale tipped them.”

For those who sought gold and found ash. For those who sought immortality and found the price.
